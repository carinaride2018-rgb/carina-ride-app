## Google Play: full description (4000 characters max)
LimoRelay is the booking and dispatch app for chauffeur and limousine companies. One app serves the whole team: dispatchers, drivers, and customers sign in to their own company's page.

FOR DISPATCHERS
- See every trip for the day on one board.
- Assign a trip to a driver with one tap.
- Watch drivers on a live map.
- Track flights so you know when a client lands.
- Send invoices and see who has paid.
- Share rides with trusted partner companies when your cars are full.

FOR DRIVERS
- See your next trip and its pickup time.
- Trip details open 70 minutes before pickup.
- Tap "On the way" only when you are driving, with a confirmation so it is never sent by accident.
- Get an alert when a new trip is assigned to you.

FOR CUSTOMERS
- Book a ride in a minute.
- See your driver's name and car.
- Get updates when your driver is on the way.

WHY COMPANIES USE LIMORELAY
- Each company has its own branded sign-in page with its own logo and name.
- Customers book through the company's page, so the client stays with the company.
- Work, trips, and balances stay in one place.

You need an account with a LimoRelay company to sign in. To start using LimoRelay for your company, visit limorelay.com.
