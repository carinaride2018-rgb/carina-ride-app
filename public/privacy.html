Privacy policy

Last updated [date]

[Company legal name] ("we") runs this booking and dispatch service for limousine and chauffeur companies. This page explains what we collect when you use the website or the mobile app, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email, mobile number and password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Trip details: pickup and drop-off addresses, dates and times, flight numbers, passenger names and notes you enter.
  • Payments: cards are entered on Stripe's secure page and held by Stripe. We keep only the card brand, last four digits and the charges made.
  • Chauffeur location: while a chauffeur is on a trip and has location turned on, the car's position is shared with dispatch and that trip's passenger. Passengers' locations are not tracked.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, a notification token from Apple or Google so we can send trip updates.
  • Photos: chauffeur, vehicle or logo photos that you choose to upload.

How we use it

To book, dispatch and complete rides; to send confirmations, reminders and trip updates by text, email and notification; to charge the card you saved for your ride; for invoices and records the limo company must keep; and to keep the service secure. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

The limo company you book with (and, if they pass your trip to a partner company, that partner) sees your trip and contact details. We use service providers to run the service: Stripe (payments), Twilio (text messages), Resend (email), Google Maps (addresses and routes), FlightAware (flight times), Apple and Google (notifications) and Render (hosting). They may use the data only to provide their service to us.

How long we keep it

Account details are kept while your account is open. Trip and payment records are kept for [7] years because limo companies need them for tax and accounting; when you delete your account, your name and contact details on those records are removed.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: in the app or on the website, open your profile and choose "Delete my account". You can also email us.
  • See or correct your details on your profile, or ask us for a copy.
  • Turn notifications and location off at any time in your phone's settings.

Children

The service is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.
